      How it works

      Callify sits between the tools you already use.

      1. 01

        Pulls transcripts straight from Gong.

      2. 02

        Analyzes every call: what was said, meant, and missed.

      3. 03

        Learns your playbook. MEDDIC, SPICED, BANT, SPIN, Challenger or your own.

      A layer on your current stack. No rip-and-replace.

      Security

      What about security?

      01

      Read-only access to Gong.

      Callify writes to HubSpot only after the rep approves.

      02

      Nothing stored beyond what’s needed.

      Transcripts processed [where and how].

      03

      A person reviews every score.

      Nothing syncs to HubSpot or Slack until a rep signs off.

      Built to clear security review, not stall in it.
